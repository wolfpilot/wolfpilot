# Hi! I'm Raz.

I'm a Fullstack Engineer with a liking for building complex things, whether that's teaching a flock of boids to behave on canvas, squeezing an entire universe into SQL tables, building an auth flow from scratch or, heck, even shaving off ten seconds from that pesky UI on a cold load.

Below is a hand-picked selection of my work. Looking for anything else? Then head over to [wolfpilot.co](https://wolfpilot.co) or reach out to me at howl@wolfpilot.co.
